EARNINGS RELEASE

Nu Holdings Ltd. Reports Fourth Quarter and Full Year 2023 Financial Results

São Paulo, Brazil, February 22, 2024 – Nu Holdings Ltd. (NYSE: NU) (“Nu” or the “Company”), one of the world’s largest digital financial services platforms, released today its Fourth Quarter 2023 financial results.

Key Highlights:
- Net Income reached $360.9 million in Q4'23, an annualized Return on Equity (ROE) of 23%, achieving one of the highest profitability levels in the region while continuing to invest heavily in growth.
- Revenues surged to $2.4 billion, growing 57% YoY FX Neutral, driven by the sustained increase in active customers and higher monetization.
- Total customers reached 93.9 million globally, cementing Nu as one of the largest and fastest-growing digital financial platforms worldwide.
- Efficiency Ratio improved to 36.0%, demonstrating the tremendous operating leverage of Nu's business model.
- Asset Quality: NPL 15-90 days stood at 4.1%, and NPL 90+ days stood at 6.1%, remaining stable and in line with management expectations despite the significant growth of the credit portfolio. 

"Our fourth quarter results crown an extraordinary year for Nu, as we continued to demonstrate the massive operating leverage of our model. We are now generating an ROE of 23% and our net income more than quintupled in the year, all while maintaining solid asset quality", said David Vélez, founder and CEO of Nubank.
